Privacy Policy

This calit instance is operated by {inject:site.operatorName}.

What calit is

calit is open-source scheduling software the operator hosts themselves. It stores all data in a database the operator controls. The maintainers of the project do not operate a central service, do not receive your data, and have no access to any deployment's database.

Data calit stores

  • Account data — your username, email address, display name, timezone, and an argon2id hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • Scheduling data — your meeting types, availability rules, and the bookings made by invitees (invitee name, email, and any answers to custom booking questions you configure).
  • Google account data — when you connect Google Calendar (optional): the Google account's email and stable subject identifier, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens. Tokens are encrypted at rest.
{#if inject:privacy.googleConfigured}

How Google user data is used

When you connect a Google account, calit requests the Google Calendar scope and uses it only to provide scheduling: it reads free/busy information from the calendars you select to compute available slots, and creates, updates, and deletes events on the write-target calendar you choose when bookings are made, rescheduled, or cancelled. calit does not read the content of your calendar events beyond busy intervals, and does not use Google data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the scheduling features you initiated.

Limited Use disclosure

calit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not transferred to third parties except as needed to provide the scheduling features, not used for advertising, and not read by humans except where required for security, to comply with law, or with your explicit consent.

Data sharing

calit does not sell or share your data.

{#if inject:privacy.googleConfigured || inject:privacy.smtpHost || inject:privacy.anyChannelConfigured || inject:privacy.oidcConfigured || inject:privacy.turnstileConfigured}

This deployment sends data to:

    {#if inject:privacy.googleConfigured}
  • Google, for the calendar operations above.
    • {/if} {#if inject:privacy.oidcConfigured}
  • The single sign-on identity provider configured by the operator, when you sign in with it.
    • {/if} {#if inject:privacy.smtpHost}
  • The SMTP server {inject:privacy.smtpHost}, which delivers booking and notification emails.
    • {/if} {#if inject:privacy.anyChannelConfigured}
  • The chat or push services hosts have configured as notification channels (for example Telegram, Slack, Discord or ntfy). Messages already delivered there cannot be recalled.
    • {/if} {#if inject:privacy.turnstileConfigured}
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks booking requests for abuse.
    • {/if}

This deployment does not send your data to any third party.

Retention and deletion

    {#if inject:privacy.googleConfigured}
  • Disconnecting a Google account in Settings → Google deletes that account's stored tokens and calendar selections from this site. It does not withdraw the grant at Google — revoke calit's access in your Google account to do that.
    • {/if}
  • Deleting your account at Settings → Delete my account removes your account, settings, meeting types, availability, bookings, connected Google accounts and notification channels.
  • A hash of a deleted account's username is kept permanently, so that name can never be re-registered and used to take over a stale login.
    • {#if inject:privacy.inviteeErasureEnabled}
  • If you booked a meeting here, the manage link in your confirmation email lets you download your data or erase it from that booking. Bookings are not linked to each other by email address, so erasure reaches only the booking whose manage link you used — not any other booking you may have made.
    • {#else}
  • If you booked a meeting here, the manage link in your confirmation email lets you download your data from that booking. This deployment has disabled self-service erasure; to have a booking erased, contact the operator.
    • {/if} {#if inject:privacy.retentionDays}
  • Booking details are anonymised {inject:privacy.retentionDays} days after the meeting ends by default on this deployment. Each host may set their own retention window instead, up to 36,500 days.
    • {#else}
  • This deployment sets no automatic retention window, so booking details are kept until removed by the host or the operator. A host may still set their own window, up to 36,500 days.
    • {/if}
  • Emails queued for sending are deleted about 30 days after they are sent (or after they were queued, if never sent). Password-reset, invitation and sign-in tokens are deleted about a day after they expire.

Security

Passwords are hashed with argon2id; Google OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. Production deployments run behind TLS with secure cookies. The operator is responsible for securing the host and database.
